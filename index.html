About

Healthcare project and clinical research professional with 6+ years of experience coordinating complex, cross-functional healthcare initiatives. Experience includes an 8-study concurrent research portfolio and implementation of a cross-institutional AYA oncology program tracking 148 patients in Year 1. Areas of expertise include project lifecycle management, process improvement, stakeholder engagement, data quality, regulatory management, metrics development, project tracking, and executive-ready reporting.
